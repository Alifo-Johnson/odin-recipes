Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 12 sheets of lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese and Parmesan cheese.
  6. Layer the lasagna noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture in a baking dish.
  7. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese on top.
  8. Bake for 25-30 minutes until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
