Egwusi

Description

Egwusi is a traditional Nigerian soup made with okra and various spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup okra, chopped
  • 1 lb fish, cleaned and cut into pieces
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1 tsp garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp palm oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the palm oil in a pot.
  2. Add the chopped onion and sauté until translucent.
  3. Add the ginger and garlic, and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they break down.
  5. Add the okra and fish, and stir to combine.
  6. Season with salt and cook for 10-15 minutes until the okra is tender.
